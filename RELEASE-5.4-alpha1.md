# WaffleHouse-Client 5.4 alpha1

Headline feature: **WaffleCast** shared Media Center sessions.

- Start/end a live broadcast from Media Center.
- Invite users from AIM or IRC with `/wafflecast` or `/wcast`.
- Native WaffleHouse Listen prompt; raw control frames stay hidden.
- Works through plaintext conversations and existing secure DM/secure-room envelopes.
- Direct multi-listener audio streaming via ffmpeg (128 kbps MP3) and mpv.
- Tokenized stream/metadata/artwork endpoints.
- WaffleCast listening is transient and does not replace the persistent local media library.
- New Album Art panel extracts and displays embedded artwork from supported audio files.
- Listener album art/title updates as the DJ changes tracks.
- Carries forward 5.3 r4 single-instance behavior and the Stop/Play + Pause/Play media fixes.
